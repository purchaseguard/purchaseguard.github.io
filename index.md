# PurchaseGuard Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

## The short version

Your shopping data, meaning your settings, wait list and savings history, stays on your own device. The only thing that goes over the internet is checking whether you have paid for PurchaseGuard, which is handled by ExtensionPay and Stripe. We never sell or share your data.

## What PurchaseGuard stores

In your browser's local extension storage, PurchaseGuard keeps your settings, such as hourly pay, monthly budget and sites you have turned it off for; your wait list, meaning item names, prices and page addresses you chose to wait on; and your history of skipped and bought items, used to show your savings and budget.

This data never leaves your device. You can erase it at any time from the PurchaseGuard dashboard, or by removing the extension.

## What PurchaseGuard reads on web pages

To spot a checkout, PurchaseGuard looks at the page address, buttons such as "Place order", and the order total shown on the page. This happens only inside your browser. It never reads or stores card numbers, passwords or other payment details.

## Payments

PurchaseGuard is free for 7 days, then costs a one-time $9.99. Payments are handled by ExtensionPay <https://extensionpay.com> and Stripe <https://stripe.com>. PurchaseGuard itself never sees your card details.

When installed, the extension gets a random ID from ExtensionPay and uses it to check whether you have paid. No shopping data is sent.

When you pay, Stripe collects your email and payment details under Stripe's privacy policy <https://stripe.com/privacy>. ExtensionPay keeps your email so you can restore your purchase on another computer.

## What PurchaseGuard does not do

PurchaseGuard has no analytics, tracking or ads. It does not send the pages you visit, prices or purchases anywhere. It does not sell or share data with anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
